Humayun's Tomb at Golden Hour

Before the Taj Mahal became the universal symbol of love, there was Humayun's Tomb. Commissioned by a grieving widow in the 16th century, this magnificent garden-tomb introduced the Persian charbagh layout to India. Walking through its massive sandstone gates at golden hour feels like stepping directly into the Mughal era.
